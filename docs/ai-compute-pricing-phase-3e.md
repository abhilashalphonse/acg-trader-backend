## Contracts

- Separate immutable pricing registry keyed by provider, exact model ID and capability descriptor ID, unique price revision, half-open effective interval [from, until), USD, source and review status. Reject overlaps, duplicate revisions, unsupported fields and invalid rates; gaps fail closed for managed credits. No live rates loaded: explicit synthetic fixture entries only; empty default catalog; runtime denied unconditionally.
- Rate strings are micro-USD per million tokens (equivalent to pico-USD per token). Multiplication and summation use BigInt, serialized as decimal integer strings in pico-USD (10^12 units per USD). No floating-point money, per-line rounding or conversion to payment cents. This is proposed accounting precision, not a purchased credit denomination. Checkout rounding, ledger storage, tax/FX and commercial margin remain separate decisions.
- Pin rate snapshot and conservative planning bound before dispatch; exact request ID, provider/model/descriptor, funding, token caps, selected rate revision and timestamp, integrity digest. Historical meter recomputes using the pinned price, never the current catalog. Digest detects corruption, not authorization; durable authenticated storage and dispatch/credit reservation remain gates.
- Split inclusive input/output into mutually exclusive standard, cached reads, cache writes (uniform or 5m/1h split) and reasoning tokens where rates explicitly differ. Inclusive rates do not add already-counted cached or reasoning tokens. Preserve cache creation counts and TTL breakdown through normalization and orchestration; never guess missing breakdown. Unknown/invalid/over-budget usage and unsupported dimensions require reconciliation; no managed debit proposal. Ambiguous provider outcome suppresses debit proposal even with reported usage. Known failed/denied/expired responses may incur provider cost; metering is separate from disclosure.
- Managed offline compute requires a pinned price before dispatch. BYO requests without a rate can continue, with explicit unknown cost and zero proposed ACG debit. BYO always has zero ACG compute debit. All metering is offline, unsettled, never a deduction. Known synthetic cost and proposed ACG debit match in the fixture only. Actual provider cost vs ACG retail price, discounts, invoices, tier/route identity, other fee dimensions, cache storage, audio/image/tool fees, currency and payment reconciliation must be reviewed separately. Additional fees are explicitly unsupported outside the no-fee synthetic model; actual provider commercial schedules are a live activation gate.
- Tests cover exact integer calculations beyond JS safe integer range, token partitioning, effective boundaries and overlap/gaps, frozen snapshots, model/funding binding, unknown usage, cache TTL splits, tamper rejection, overrun, BYO no debit, managed price admission, ambiguous outcome and adapter/orchestrator/verification regressions.

## Activation boundary

No real price catalog, commercial markup, credit ledger, payment, charge, inference or production route. Accounting amounts are synthetic proposals, not an invoice or payable balance. Phase 3F must implement durable reservation, single-settlement/idempotency and reconciliation before any managed dispatch. Production activation requires approved price versions, provider-specific live usage/invoice verification, permissions and release acceptance.
